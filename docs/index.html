Why buyers come to House Of Wax

Real listings, real detail

Condition notes, pressing details, and photos of the actual item — not stock images — so you know exactly what you're getting.

Direct communication

Ask a seller a question before you commit, or make an offer. Nothing about a listing has to stay a mystery.

Real trust signals

Seller and buyer standing is based on actual completed transactions and reviews, not a made-up rating.

Why sellers open a store here

You get paid directly by the buyer through PayPal — House Of Wax never holds your money. You control your own store: photos, pricing, condition notes, and your own seller profile and story. Full details on how it all works are in your account once you sign up.
