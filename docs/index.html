How it works

Getting started takes a few minutes, whether you're buying one record or working through a longer list.

01

Browse listings

Search by artist or genre, or look through what's new. Listings include real photos and condition notes, not stock images.

02

Message the seller

Ask about condition, pressing details, or anything else you'd like to know before you buy. You're talking to the person who owns it.

03

Pay securely, get your order

Payment goes directly to the seller through PayPal — House Of Wax doesn't hold or process funds. Your order ships from there.

What buyers get

Detailed listings

Condition notes, pressing details, and photos of the actual item — not stock images — so you know what you're buying.

Direct communication

You can message a seller with questions, or make an offer, before deciding to buy.

Trust based on history

Seller and buyer standing reflects completed transactions and reviews, not a self-reported rating.

Why sellers open a store here

You get paid directly by the buyer through PayPal — House Of Wax never holds your money. You control your own store: photos, pricing, condition notes, and your own seller profile and story. Full details on how it all works are in your account once you sign up.

Start Selling

Setting up takes minutes

Apply from your House Of Wax account, add your first listing, and you're live in your own store — no separate seller platform to learn.
